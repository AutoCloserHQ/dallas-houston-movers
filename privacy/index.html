Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this site.

DallasHoustonMovers.com is operated by ModelMoving. This policy explains the types of information that may be collected through the site, how that information may be used, and the choices available to visitors and moving-quote requesters.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you use a moving quote request form or otherwise interact with DallasHoustonMovers.com.

Information you provide

  • Name, phone number, and email address.
  • Moving origin and destination.
  • Estimated move date and move size.
  • Information about inventory, packing, stairs, elevators, parking, storage, specialty items, extra stops, timing, or other move-related details you choose to provide.

Technical and usage information

We may also receive information about how visitors reach and use the site, such as page views, referral source, campaign information, device or browser information, and similar website-usage data.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and review moving quote requests.
  • Understand the route, timing, availability, and move details associated with a request.
  • Help connect customers with selected moving partners that may be appropriate for the request.
  • Allow moving partners to discuss service details, availability, and pricing.
  • Operate, maintain, analyze, and improve DallasHoustonMovers.com and related ModelMoving resources.
  • Measure website traffic, form activity, referral sources, and marketing performance.
  • Prevent misuse, protect the site, troubleshoot problems, and support site operations.

3. How Information May Be Shared

When you submit a moving quote request, information may be shared with selected moving partners so they can review the route, timing, availability, move details, and potential service needs.

Information may also be processed by service providers that support site operations, including form-processing, website hosting, analytics, communication, and related technical services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, enforce site terms, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or support a business transition involving ModelMoving or this site.

Submitting a quote request does not obligate you to purchase moving services.

4. Analytics, Cookies & Similar Technologies

DallasHoustonMovers.com uses website analytics tools, including Google Analytics, to better understand site usage, traffic sources, page activity, and conversion events.

Analytics and related technologies may use cookies, browser storage, device identifiers, or similar mechanisms to recognize a browser or device and collect usage information.

Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies. Doing so may affect certain website functions or the accuracy of analytics measurement.

5. Communications About Your Moving Request

When you submit a moving quote request, you agree that ModelMoving and selected moving partners may contact you about that request by phone, text, or email.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive communications is not required to purchase services.

If you no longer want to receive a particular communication, you may use any available opt-out method in that communication or tell the sender that you no longer wish to be contacted.

6. Security & Data Retention

Reasonable administrative, technical, and operational measures may be used to help protect information handled through the site. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support quote requests, customer follow-up, site operations, recordkeeping, analytics, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and other legitimate business or legal purposes.

7. Your Choices & Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information associated with your use of the site.

You may also ask questions about information connected to a moving quote request or request that future communications about that request stop.

Some information may need to be retained when required or permitted for legal, security, fraud-prevention, recordkeeping, or operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

DallasHoustonMovers.com is intended for people arranging or researching moving services and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek moving quote request information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in site operations, technology, services, or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

10. Contact & ModelMoving

DallasHoustonMovers.com is operated by ModelMoving, a moving quote request and matching platform that helps connect customers with selected moving partners based on route, availability, timing, and move details.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or information submitted through the site, you can contact ModelMoving through its primary website.
